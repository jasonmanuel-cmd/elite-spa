Ceramic Coatings

Ceramic Coatings in Boerne & San Antonio

A liquid polymer that cures to a hard, hydrophobic layer — adding depth and gloss while helping water and grime release more easily during washing.

Call (830) 431-2088
Why It Matters

What a ceramic coating offers

  • Hydrophobic surface

    Water tends to bead and sheet, which can make routine washing easier.

  • Enhanced gloss

    Adds a deep, reflective finish that many owners love on darker paint.

  • Easier maintenance

    Contaminants have a harder time bonding, so cleanup is often simpler.

  • UV & chemical resistance

    Helps guard against sun exposure and everyday environmental fallout.

Good to Know

Ceramic Coatings questions

How long do ceramic coatings last?

Longevity depends on the product, layers, use, and maintenance. Manufacturers publish durability ranges; we'll confirm the expected lifespan of the coating we apply.

Does a coating stop rock chips?

No. Ceramic coatings add gloss and make cleaning easier but are not designed to stop rock chips. For impact protection, consider paint protection film.

Do I still need to wash my car?

Yes. A coating makes washing easier but regular maintenance is still important. We'll provide simple care guidance.

Should paint be corrected first?

Often, yes. Correcting swirls before coating helps lock in a clean, glossy finish rather than sealing in imperfections.
