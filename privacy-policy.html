Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Elite Auto Spa (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a trade name of Elite Mobile Detail LLC, 108 Scheele Rd, Unit 3, Boerne, TX 78015. This policy explains what information we collect through eliteautospasa.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our quote form we collect the service you are interested in; your vehicle's year, make, model, and type; any project details you write; your name, phone number, and email address; your preferred contact method and timeframe; and whether you agreed to receive text messages.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not currently run advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request and prepare a quote.
  • To schedule and carry out services, and to send appointment updates.
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Who handles your information

We use service providers who process information only on our behalf: Vercel (website hosting), and Resend (delivery of quote-request emails to us). The contact page embeds a Google Map and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when those load. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Text messaging

If you check the text-message consent box, we use your mobile number to send appointment updates and service-related messages. Your mobile number and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our SMS Terms for how to opt out.

Retention and security

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as needed to provide services, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize them. We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) transmission, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you submitted, and you may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Texas and other state laws may give you additional rights; contact us and we will respond as the law requires.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email contact@eliteautospasa.com or call (830) 431-2088.
